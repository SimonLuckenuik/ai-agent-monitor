# The CPU runs at 160 MHz

Date: 2026-10-25

Spec: [007](../../specs/007-big-tiles-and-line-font/SPEC.md)

## Evidence

The line font and SVG paths are float maths in software (no FPU). On the clock at
80 MHz the Big tiles drew glyph by glyph and Big date tripped the software watchdog.
`/status` `drawMs` was added to measure; strokes were made cheaper (only pixels near a
segment, square roots on edge pixels only, a yield every row).

## Decision

`board_build.f_cpu = 160000000`: roughly halves CPU-bound drawing. The ESP8266 is rated
for 160 MHz; the cost is a little more power and heat. The emulator's timing follows
`f_cpu`.
