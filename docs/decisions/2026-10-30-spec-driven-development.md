# Spec-driven development

Date: 2026-10-30

## Decision

Every new piece of work starts as `specs/NNN-<slug>/` with SPEC, PLAN, RESEARCH and
PROGRESS, and is approved by the owner before implementation. Specs and plans are kept
true as decisions change. Decision records, issue records, the roadmap and STATUS.md are
updated along the way. The rules are in CLAUDE.md.

Specs 001-017 were written after the fact from the history of the fork.
